## 1. Executive Summary & Statutory Authority

To accelerate Bangladesh's clean energy transition and alleviate industrial power shortages, the Government of Bangladesh, through the **National Board of Revenue (NBR)** and the **Sustainable and Renewable Energy Development Authority (SREDA)** under the Ministry of Power, Energy and Mineral Resources, has enacted landmark statutory regulatory orders:

1. **SRO No. 159-Law/2026/14/Customs** (Issued June 08, 2026):
   - Enacted under Section 25(1) of the *Customs Act 2023*, Section 126(1) of the *Value Added Tax & Supplementary Duty Act 2012*, and the *Income Tax Act 2023*.
   - Exempts approved solar components from Customs Duty (CD), Regulatory Duty (RD), and Supplementary Duty (SD).
2. **SRO No. 241-Law/2026/71/Customs** (Issued June 30, 2026):
   - Clarifies eligible beneficiary categories:
     - **VAT-Compliant Manufacturing Enterprises** installing rooftop solar for self-consumption and net-metering.
     - **Renewable Energy Service Companies (RESCO)** installing solar under commercial PPAs.
3. **SRO No. 338-Law/2026/88/Customs** (Issued September 16, 2026):
   - Slashes the residual 15% VAT and 2% Advance Income Tax (AIT), reducing the **total import tax burden to just 1%** for qualifying consignments.
4. **SREDA Solar Components Quality Standards (যন্ত্রাংশের মানমাত্রা)**:
   - Prescribes mandatory Bangladesh Standards (BDS) and International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC) test certifications required for customs clearance.

## 3. Mandatory SREDA & BSTI Quality Standards (যন্ত্রাংশের মানমাত্রা)

Under Section 3 of SRO 159, duty exemptions are **strictly contingent** upon providing certified test reports from internationally accredited testing laboratories (e.g., TÜV Rheinland, TÜV SÜD, UL, SGS, Intertek).

### A. Solar PV Modules:
1. **BDS IEC 61215 (Parts 1, 1-1, 1-2, 1-3, 2)**:
   - Terrestrial photovoltaic (PV) modules — Design qualification and type approval.
2. **BDS IEC 61730 (Parts 1 & 2)**:
   - Photovoltaic module safety qualification (Requirements for construction & testing).
3. **IEC 61701**:
   - Salt mist corrosion testing (Mandatory for coastal zones e.g., Chattogram, Khulna, Cox's Bazar).
4. **IEC TS 62804**:
   - Detection of Potential-Induced Degradation (PID resistance certification).
5. **BDS IEC 60891**:
   - Procedures for temperature and irradiance corrections to measured I-V characteristics.

### B. Solar Inverters (String / Hybrid / Central):
1. **BDS IEC 62109-1 & IEC 62109-2**:
   - Safety of power converters used in photovoltaic power systems.
2. **BDS IEC 61727 / IEC 62116**:
   - Utility interconnection characteristics and mandatory anti-islanding protection.
3. **IEC 61000 Series**:
   - Electromagnetic Compatibility (EMC) emissions and harmonic distortion limits.

### C. Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS / LiFePO4):
1. **IEC 62619**:
   - Safety requirements for secondary lithium cells and batteries for industrial stationary applications.
2. **IEC 62485-2 / UN 38.3**:
   - Safety requirements for secondary batteries, electrical installations, and transport clearance.

### D. DC Solar Cables:
1. **EN 50618 / IEC 62930**:
   - Electric cables for photovoltaic systems with cross-linked polyolefin insulation, rated for 1,500V DC.

## 4. End-to-End Import Execution SOP (SREDA to ASYCUDA Port Clearance)

To ensure smooth customs clearance through Chittagong Port, Dhaka Airport Cargo, or Benapole Land Port without demurrage or duty reassessments, follow this 5-step SOP:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ STEP 1: PROFORMA INVOICE (PI) FORMULATION                                                       │
│ • Ensure supplier's PI displays exact 8-digit HS Code: Modules (8541.43.00), Inverters (8504.40.90)  │
│ • Product description must match SREDA terminology word-for-word.                               │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ STEP 2: BANK LETTER OF CREDIT (L/C) OPENING                                                     │
│ • Instruct AD Bank trade desk to insert mandatory SRO 159/2026 clause into Field 47A.            │
│ • Require manufacturer to supply original TÜV/UL certificates and factory flash test data.     │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ STEP 3: SREDA ONLINE NOC APPLICATION                                                             │
│ • Submit import application via SREDA portal (https://sreda.gov.bd) with:                       │
│   a) Copy of L/C & Commercial Invoice                                                            │
│   b) Single Line Diagram (SLD) & Plant Layout                                                   │
│   c) International test lab certificates matching serial numbers                                 │
│   d) VAT-compliant manufacturing certificate / RESCO PPA contract                                │
│ • SREDA issues official Import Recommendation NOC within 5–7 working days.                      │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ STEP 4: ASYCUDA WORLD CUSTOMS ENTRY (BOX 44)                                                    │
│ • Direct C&F Agent to input SRO 159 exemption code into ASYCUDA World.                          │
│ • Input official SREDA NOC approval number into Box 44 (Licensing/Permits).                     │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ STEP 5: PHYSICAL INSPECTION & PORT RELEASE                                                      │
│ • Customs appraiser verifies container serials against SREDA NOC and packing list.              │
│ • Zero Customs Duty (0%), zero VAT, and 1% AIT applied. Release order issued.                   │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
