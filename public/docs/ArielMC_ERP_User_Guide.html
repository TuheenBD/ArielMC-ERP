2. Sector Product Master & Costing Margin Engine

The Product Master catalog serves as the single source of truth for all products engineered and sourced through ArielMC. It unifies technical product specifications, international Harmonized System (HS) customs codes, factory ex-work purchase prices, and locked ArielMC sourcing margins.

2.1 The Four Core Manufacturing Sectors

  • Leather Goods: Full-grain vegetable-tanned bovine leather, chrome-free aniline finishes, solid brass hardware. Audited under LWG Gold standard.
  • Jute & Biodegradable Crafts: 100% natural golden jute fiber, handwoven braided weaves, certified organic cotton linings.
  • Apparel & Sustainable Textiles: Heavyweight organic cotton french terry, mercerized pique, OEKO-TEX Standard 100 and GOTS organic certified.
  • Artisan Ceramics & Tableware: High-fired porcelain, fine bone china, reactive glazes. LFGB and US FDA food contact safe.

2.2 Costing Margin Calculation Protocol

ArielMC calculates FOB target pricing via the standard formula:

FOB_Target_USD = Factory_ExWorks_USD + (Factory_ExWorks_USD × Ariel_Sourcing_Margin_Pct) + Inland_Bonded_Logistics_USD

All margins are contractually fixed between 18.0% and 35.0%, ensuring competitive landed buyer pricing while guaranteeing sustainable margins across our operating stations.

4. R&D, Tech Packs & SDO Sampling Protocol

Before bulk production, every product progresses through the ArielMC Five-Stage Sample Development Order (SDO) lifecycle.

Stage Stage Name Objective & Deliverables Turnaround
Stage 1 Lab Dip / Material Swatch Color matching, pantone lab dips (Light D65), physical leather strike-offs. 5 Business Days
Stage 2 Proto Sample First 3D dimensional mock-up verifying silhouette and pattern ergonomics. 8 Business Days
Stage 3 Fit / Size Set Sample Grading verification across complete size run (S to XXL or sizing tiers). 10 Business Days
Stage 4 Pre-Production (PP) Sample Created on actual production lines using bulk raw material allocations. 12 Business Days
Stage 5 Golden Seal (Approved) Signed, sealed master sample counter-stamped by both buyer and ArielMC. Contract Gate

5. Two-Way Order Management System (OMS)

ArielMC utilizes a proprietary Two-Way Order Execution Model. When an export Sales Order is finalized with an international retail buyer:

  1. Buyer Contract Gate: Buyer issues Letter of Credit (L/C) or Sales Contract at the agreed FOB landed rate.
  2. Back-to-Back PO Issuance: ArielMC BD automatically issues a back-to-back Bangladesh Factory Purchase Order at the pre-negotiated Ex-Works rate, locking raw material commitments.
  3. Bonded Allocation: Factory registers raw hide or yarn consumption declarations under ArielMC's bonded warehouse quota.

6. Logistics, Bonded Warehousing & Bangladesh Customs

ArielMC oversees international ocean and air freight logistics connecting Bangladesh ports to destination hubs worldwide.

  • Ocean Freight (Chattogram Port - CGP): Primary export terminal for bulk containers (20ft / 40ft HC). Transit: 22-26 days to Southampton/Felixstowe (UK), 28-32 days to New York/New Jersey (USA).
  • Air Freight (Hazrat Shahjalal Int'l Airport - DAC): High-priority express consignments, fast-fashion replenishment, and Golden Seal courier parcels via DHL / FedEx.
  • Customs Clearance (C&F): Automated processing of Bill of Lading, Packing Lists, Certificate of Origin (Export Promotion Bureau - EPB), and GSP Form A.

7. Bonded Inventory & Raw Material Transfers

Under ArielMC BD's National Board of Revenue (NBR) Bond License (NBR-BOND-2024-C882), imported premium raw materials (Italian veg-tan hides, Swiss dyes, YKK zippers) are stored duty-free at Bonded Depot A-4 (Chattogram).

Statutory Rule: Raw Material Utilization Declaration (UD)
Every square foot of imported leather and kilogram of organic yarn is tracked against authorized Bangladesh BGMEA / LFMEAB Utilization Declarations. Unauthorized domestic release carries severe statutory penalties.

8. Multi-Currency Finance & Mushak 6.3 Tax Engine

ArielMC ERP provides automated multi-station financial consolidation, FX hedging, and National Board of Revenue (NBR) VAT compliance.

8.1 Mushak 6.3 VAT Tax Invoicing Standards

Under the Bangladesh Value Added Tax and Supplementary Duty Act 2012, all commercial dispatches require an official Mushak 6.3 tax invoice:

  • Deemed Exports: Zero-rated (0% VAT) when backed by an export L/C or authorized bonded supply contract.
  • Domestic Institutional Supply: Standard 15% VAT plus applicable supplementary duties.

9. Enterprise Security, User Profiles & 2SA

To protect sensitive pricing, buyer relationships, and financial records, ArielMC enforces strict security layers:

  • Two-Step Authentication (2SA): All staff must enable 2SA via TOTP Authenticator (Google Authenticator) or encrypted WhatsApp OTP to +8801819273458.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):
    • Managing Director: Unrestricted global access, entity station configuration, and financial approval gates.
    • VP of Global Sourcing: Product master creation, factory allocation, and SDO approvals.
    • Chief Compliance Auditor: Factory audit scoring, ETP verification, and compliance flag toggles.
    • Senior Merchandiser: SDO sample tracking, courier dispatch, and buyer RFQ conversion.
